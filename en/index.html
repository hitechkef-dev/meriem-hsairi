Your questions,

How do I know which treatment is right for me? +

The choice depends on your anatomy, skin, medical history and goals. A consultation helps assess the indication and possible alternatives.

Are results immediate? +

It depends on the procedure. Some results appear quickly while others develop progressively. Expected timelines are explained during consultation.

How many sessions will I need? +

The number of sessions varies by treatment and individual response. A personalized protocol is defined after assessment.
